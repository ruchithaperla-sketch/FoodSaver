How It Works

A simple process to make a big difference in our community.

1

Register

Sign up as a Donor to list excess, high-quality surplus food from your kitchen or store.

2

List Food

Easily post surplus food items detailing servings, categories, location, and photos.

3

Rescue

Volunteers and local NGOs coordinate pickup and take the food to those in need.

1

Register

Create an account as a verified NGO to search and request surplus food donations.

2

Request

Claim food listings. Volunteers are auto-assigned to transport items directly to you.

3

Confirm Receipt

Confirm delivery receipt on your dashboard once volunteers deliver the food.

1

Register

Sign up as a Volunteer driver/courier to access active pending rescue tasks.

2

Claim Deliveries

Accept tasks, route using mapping coordinates, and pick up food packages from Donors.

3

Complete Rescues

Mark tasks as transit and delivered on delivery completion to finalize the rescue.
